Definitions & Contexts (2 Senses)

Cambridge / Oxford English
1 Noun B2 [Countable / Uncountable]

A subject or problem that people are thinking and talking about.

Vấn đề, đề tài tranh luận hoặc lo ngại quan trọng.

Example

“We need to address these environmental issues before it’s too late.”

2 Verb B2 [Transitive] (issuing, issued)

To produce or provide something official, such as a statement, permit, passport, or warning.

Phát hành, cấp (giấy phép, hộ chiếu, thông báo chính thức).

Example

“The office will be issuing permits starting from next Monday.”

Phrasal Verbs & Common Idioms

take issue with (sb/sth) Idiom

To disagree strongly with someone or have an objection.

“I take issue with some of the points in this report.”

at issue Idiom

Most important in a discussion or debate; under consideration.

“The point at issue is what is best for the children.”

make an issue of (sth) Phrase

To treat something as important when it really isn't.

“Don't make an issue of it; it was just a small misunderstanding.”

issue forth Phrasal V.

Formal: to come out of or emerge from a place.
